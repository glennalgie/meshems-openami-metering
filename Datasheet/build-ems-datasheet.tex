\vspace{7pt}
{\small
Deploying EV charge/discharge with rooftop solar PV and wind on an existing building?
\textbf{BUILD-EMS} is the open-source behind-the-meter controller that integrates
\textbf{bidirectional DC/AC inverters}, multi-tenant ``plug-in solar'', and EV infrastructure
into one balanced building minigrid --- enforcing multi-zone energy policy while protecting
the mains.

\smallskip
\textbf{\color{tpdark}What Sets BUILD-EMS Apart:}
\begin{arrows}
\item \textbf{Bidirectional \& Inverter-Ready} : coordinates DC/AC bidirectional inverters and
converters for solar PV, wind and battery, with EV charge and discharge (V2X).
\item \textbf{Multi-Tenant ``Plug-in Solar''} : partitions shared rooftop generation and storage
across tenants with per-zone, time-of-day energy policies.
\item \textbf{Mains-Protective} : balances the building mains and responds to grid-peak DERMS
events without exceeding the service limit.
\item \textbf{Open-Source Hardware} : released under CERN-OHL-S v2 --- schematics, board files
and firmware published on GitHub (meshEMS / X-EMS).
\item \textbf{Field-Serviceable} : built from open, off-the-shelf commodity modules, snap-in
mounted for fast in-country assembly and repair.
\end{arrows}

\smallskip
BUILD-EMS turns a building rooftop into a coordinated, multi-tenant minigrid --- maximizing
solar, wind and EV flexibility while keeping the mains within limits.
}
